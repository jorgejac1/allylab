# AllyLab Cookie Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

## What Data We Collect

The AllyLab Cookie Capture extension collects:
- **Session cookies** from the active browser tab when you click "Capture Cookies"
- **Extension settings** (dashboard URL, API URL, scan preferences) stored in Chrome sync storage

## How We Use Your Data

- Cookie data is transmitted **only** to your configured AllyLab dashboard instance
- Cookie data is used **solely** for authenticated accessibility scanning
- No data is sent to Anthropic, AllyLab servers, or any third party
- Extension settings are synced via Chrome's built-in sync storage

## Data Storage

- Captured cookies are stored temporarily in your browser's clipboard or local storage
- Cookies are not persisted beyond the active session
- Extension settings are stored in Chrome sync storage and follow Chrome's data policies

## Third-Party Services

This extension does **not** communicate with any third-party services. All data stays between your browser and your self-hosted AllyLab instance.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Clear all extension data via Chrome settings
- Uninstall the extension at any time to remove all stored data
- Configure which AllyLab instance receives your data

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at: https://github.com/allylab/allylab

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the extension's store listing.
